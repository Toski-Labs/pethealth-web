## Who we are

Toski Labs is the app lab behind Pawta and is responsible for the data described here. Contact: [toskilabs@gmail.com](mailto:toskilabs@gmail.com).

## What we collect

Only what the app needs to work and, on the free plan, to show ads:

- **Account**
  The name and email Apple or Google share when you sign in. If you hide your email with Apple, we only get a relay address.
- **Your pets**
  What you enter: name, species, breed, birth date, weight, vaccines, medicines, vet visits, reminders and contacts.
- **Subscription**
  Your plan status, as reported by Apple. We never receive your card details.
- **Diagnostics**
  Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and usage statistics without an advertising identifier (Google Analytics for Firebase).
- **Ads**
  Free plan only. The Google AdMob SDK collects technical device identifiers, your interactions with ads and, if you allow it, the iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA). Details are in the Ads section.

## How we use it

To store and sync your data across devices, send the reminders you set up, unlock your plan’s features, fix crashes, answer you when you ask for help and, on the free plan, show ads.

We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use your pets’ information for advertising.

## Where your data lives

The app keeps everything on your iPhone first. A copy is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), on servers in São Paulo, Brazil, so you don’t lose anything when you switch devices. Reminders are scheduled on the iPhone itself.

## Ads

On the free plan, with or without an account, the app shows subtle ads, never on the home screen, in health actions, in forms or during the app’s initial setup. The iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only for ads, and only if you allow it. Other technical device identifiers go to Google to show and measure the ads. Plus subscribers see no ads.

- **Ad network**
  Ads come from Google AdMob. Google handles this data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and explains [how it uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **What goes to Google**
  Technical device identifiers, to show and measure the ads, and your interactions with them, such as seeing or tapping one. We never send your pets’ data or any health data.
- **iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA)**
  Used only for ads, and only if you allow it in the iOS tracking prompt.
- **Consent notice**
  After the app’s initial setup, the first time an ad is about to show, the app shows Apple’s tracking prompt and, when the law in your country requires it, Google’s consent notice.
- **Changing your choice**
  Tracking: in iPhone Settings, under Privacy & Security › Tracking › Pawta. Consent: anytime, in the app, under Settings › Ad privacy.

## Who we share it with

The services that make the app work and, on the free plan, our advertising partner:

- **Google (Firebase)**
  Sign-in, database, crash reports and statistics.
- **Apple**
  Sign in with Apple, purchases and subscriptions.
- **Your calendar**
  If you turn on export (Plus), events go to the iPhone Calendar or Google Calendar you connected.
- **Google AdMob (advertising partner)**
  Free plan only, to show and measure ads. It isn’t needed for the app to work. Details are in the Ads section above.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Items you delete are permanently removed within 30 days. When you delete your account, we erase everything within 30 days.

## Your rights

Under Brazil’s LGPD (and similar laws where you live), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to know who it’s shared with.

- **Delete your account**
  Right in the app, under Settings › Account › Delete account.
- **Request a copy or ask a question**
  Email [toskilabs@gmail.com](mailto:toskilabs@gmail.com).

## This website

The Pawta website (pawta.toski-labs.com.br) has no sign-in and uses no cookies, analytics or advertising tools.

- **Your preferences**
  The theme and language you choose are saved only in your browser, so the site remembers them next time. They never leave your device.
- **Special dates**
  To know when to show the look for dates like Halloween and Christmas, the site checks Firebase Remote Config (Google) at most twice a day, after the page loads. For that, Firebase creates a technical identifier in your browser, with no link to you or to your app account. The answer is saved only in your browser.
- **Hosting**
  The site runs on Firebase Hosting (Google), which may log technical access data, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the site running.

## Children

Pawta is made for adults who look after pets. It isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you think a child has created an account, email [toskilabs@gmail.com](mailto:toskilabs@gmail.com) and we’ll delete the data.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top and let you know in the app when the change matters.
